# CS 370 – Current and Emerging Trends in Computer Science

## Pirate Intelligent Agent – Deep Q-Learning

### Project Overview

For this project, I developed an intelligent pirate agent that uses reinforcement learning and neural networks to navigate a maze and find treasure. The goal was to train the agent to make decisions based on rewards, penalties, and previous experiences rather than following a predetermined path.

### What Code Was Provided, and What Did I Create?

The project included starter code that defined the maze environment and experience replay functionality through the `TreasureMaze.py` and `GameExperience.py` files. These components provided the environment and tools necessary for the agent to interact with the maze and store experiences.

My responsibility was to implement and train the intelligent agent using deep Q-learning. I worked with the neural network, training process, and exploration strategy to help the pirate learn effective paths to the treasure. I also adjusted the epsilon decay rate to allow more exploration during training and tested the model's performance. During my final training run, the agent achieved a 100% recent win rate and successfully passed the maze completion check.

## Reflection

### What do computer scientists do, and why does it matter?

Computer scientists design, develop, and improve technology to solve problems. Their work involves programming, analyzing data, developing algorithms, and finding efficient solutions to complex challenges. This matters because technology affects nearly every part of our lives, from communication and healthcare to security and automation. Throughout this course, I learned how artificial intelligence can help systems recognize patterns, learn from experience, and make decisions.

### How do I approach a problem as a computer scientist?

I approach problems by first understanding the requirements, identifying possible solutions, and breaking larger tasks into smaller steps. I then develop and test my code, analyze the results, and make improvements when necessary. During this project, I applied this process by evaluating the pirate agent's training performance and adjusting its exploration strategy. This experience reinforced the importance of testing, troubleshooting, and understanding why a solution works rather than simply getting the code to run.

### What are my ethical responsibilities to the end user and the organization?

As a computer scientist, I have a responsibility to develop technology that is reliable, secure, and fair. This includes protecting user information, recognizing potential biases, and considering how automated decisions might affect people. Throughout this course, I learned that AI systems can produce unintended results depending on their training data, reward structures, and design. I believe it is important to evaluate these risks, maintain transparency, and ensure that technology benefits users while meeting the organization's needs.
